Mathematics Fundamentals

A Practical Foundation
Part II: Introduction to Algebra
By Claude Sonnet 4.5, Claude Opus 4.5, and Jim Hamilton
Version 1.0 — January 2026

Part II builds on the arithmetic foundations of Part I, introducing the powerful ideas of algebra. You'll learn to work with variables, solve equations, graph relationships, and use mathematical language to describe patterns and solve real-world problems.

Each section includes clear explanations, worked examples, and practice problems with full solutions. The material progresses systematically from basic concepts to more advanced topics, with each section building on what came before.
